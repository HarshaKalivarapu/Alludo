    How to play

    Find the secret word. You have unlimited guesses.

    Every guess gets a number: its place in a list of about 40,000 words, sorted by how closely each one connects to the secret word. The secret word is #1.

    • 1–300 very warm
    • 301–1,500 warm
    • 1,501+ cold

    "Close" means connected the way people think: things found together, parts, places, people, and other meanings count, not just synonyms. Plurals and verb forms count as their base word, so "skates" is "skate".

    The board changes color as your best guess reaches each zone: brick when you're cold, amber when you're warm, green chalkboard when you're close. It never goes back.

    A new word arrives every day at midnight Eastern time.

    Give up?

    You'll see the secret word and the game ends. You can still guess afterward, but it won't count.
